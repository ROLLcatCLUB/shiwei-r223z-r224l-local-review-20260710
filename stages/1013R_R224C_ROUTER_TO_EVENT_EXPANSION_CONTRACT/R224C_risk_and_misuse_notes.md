# R224C Risk And Misuse Notes

stage_id: 1013R_R224C_ROUTER_TO_EVENT_EXPANSION_CONTRACT

## Risk 1. Router becomes decorative

If router values are recorded but do not change event fields, the contract fails.

Control:

Every router value must affect at least one classroom_event_expansion field.

## Risk 2. High support becomes high lecture

Control:

teacher_support_density affects circulation, checkpoint, rescue strategy, and evidence capture before it affects explanation density.

## Risk 3. Teacher default manuscript shows backend fields

Control:

Teacher default manuscript must naturalize router effects into rhythm and process. Raw router field names belong only in review ledger.

## Risk 4. Technique preparation becomes product pressure

Control:

For technique_preparation, trial sample and method comparison are valid evidence. A polished final artwork is not always required.

## Risk 5. Intro understanding becomes thin

Control:

Low or medium practice does not mean shallow. It still needs strong observation, comparison, naming, and understanding evidence.

## Risk 6. Screen/sheet/evidence rules are generic

Control:

Screen, learning sheet, and evidence expectations must vary by unit_phase_role.

## Boundary

R224C must not modify R223M/N/O manuscripts, R222D component library, R97B, frontend/backend, prompt, model, runtime, or database. It must not publish v0.2, create HTML, write back lesson body, or formal apply.
