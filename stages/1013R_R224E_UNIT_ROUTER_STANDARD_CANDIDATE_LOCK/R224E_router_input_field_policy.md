## Teacher Default Policy

These fields must not appear as raw labels in teacher default manuscripts.

They may only appear as naturalized teaching rhythm, such as:

- this lesson is mainly for observation and understanding;
- this lesson gives students more hands-on creation time;
- this lesson uses short trials before later formal work;
- this lesson needs teacher circulation and process evidence.
