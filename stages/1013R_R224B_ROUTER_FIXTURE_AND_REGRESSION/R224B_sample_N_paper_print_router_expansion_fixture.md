## Expected Classroom Expansion Effect

This sample should not become a heavy finished-product lesson too early.

Expected shifts:

- material observation and technique demonstration are visible but not excessive;
- trial samples and method comparison are central;
- final work is a modest carrier for print-mark understanding;
- learning sheet evidence records prediction, trial result, and print-mark features;
- teacher support focuses on method bottlenecks: too much color, uneven pressure, fast peeling, weak texture;
- showcase focuses on explaining paper/material/method/effect, not only on beautiful product.

## Regression Pass Signal

The fixture passes if the expansion clearly reads as a middle-unit technique-preparation lesson with controlled practice and method evidence, not as a final project lesson.
