## Expected Classroom Expansion Effect

This sample should not become a heavy creation lesson or a pure science experiment.

Expected shifts:

- life color observation and naming come first;
- color mixing is a micro-practice used to understand difference, not an end in itself;
- demonstration stays at color relationship and method safety;
- final expression remains light enough for an early-unit understanding lesson;
- evidence focuses on color observation, color trial, naming, and visual expression;
- teacher language keeps art terms such as color block, warm/cool tendency, brightness, color feeling, and image association.

## Regression Pass Signal

The fixture passes if the expansion clearly reads as an early-unit visual-language understanding lesson, not a heavy painting product lesson and not a science-only color-mixing experiment.
