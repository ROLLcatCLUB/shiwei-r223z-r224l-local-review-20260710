Control:

The stationery fixture makes teacher support heavy but explanation only medium. Support means circulation, rescue, and evidence capture, not more lecture.

### Risk 2. Technique preparation becomes final product pressure

Control:

The paper print fixture keeps trial sample, print method comparison, and learning sheet evidence central. Finished work remains modest.

### Risk 3. Intro understanding becomes heavy creation

Control:

The color collision fixture keeps observation, naming, color trial, and brief expression central. It does not require a full painting product.

### Risk 4. All router effects collapse into low/medium/high

Control:

Control:

Teacher default view impact check requires natural manuscript expression and hides raw router field names.

### Risk 6. Regression mutates canonical manuscripts

Control:

R224B is fixture and regression only. Existing R223M/N/O teacher manuscripts are not edited.
