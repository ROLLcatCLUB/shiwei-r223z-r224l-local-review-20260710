# R224A Teacher Support Density Rules

stage_id: 1013R_R224A_UNIT_LEVEL_PRACTICE_INTENSITY_ROUTER_PLANNING

## Definition

`teacher_support_density` describes how much teacher scaffolding, observation, rescue, and feedback should be generated.

## Light

Use when:

- task is simple;
- students mainly observe, classify, compare, or express briefly;
- excessive scaffolding would over-teach.

Support style:

- one or two guiding questions;
- short summary;
- minimal evidence check.

## Normal

Use when:

- students need guided practice or method comparison;
- teacher needs to prepare common misconceptions and short rescue strategies.

Support style:

- demonstration points;
- common student response predictions;
- one or two rescue moves;
- learning sheet evidence.

## Heavy

Use when:

- students perform sustained creation, design, collaboration, or revision;
- common failure points can block completion;
- performance task evidence matters.

Support style:

- circulation observation checklist;
- material / technique rescue strategy;
- student grouping or division of labor notes;
- process evidence capture;
- mid-process comparison and revision prompts.

## Guard

Heavy support is not the same as heavy teacher talk. In practice-heavy lessons, support often means better observation and timely intervention, not more explanation.
