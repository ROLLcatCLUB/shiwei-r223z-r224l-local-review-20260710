# R224A Practice Intensity Decision Rules

stage_id: 1013R_R224A_UNIT_LEVEL_PRACTICE_INTENSITY_ROUTER_PLANNING

## Definition

`practice_intensity` describes how central hands-on student practice is to the lesson's teaching responsibility.

It is not a quality score. A low-practice lesson can be valid if its responsibility is understanding, appreciation, observation, or unit framing.

## Low

Use `low` when:

- the lesson mainly introduces concepts, questions, artworks, life examples, or appreciation objects;
- student output is mostly observation record, oral expression, sorting, or simple note;
- heavy creation would distract from the lesson responsibility.

Expansion effect:

- explanation density: medium;
- demonstration density: low or medium;
- micro-practice count: 0-1;
- formal creation time: low;
- evidence: observation record, vocabulary, comparison note, oral statement.

## Medium

Use `medium` when:

- the lesson requires material/technique trials, controlled experiments, short visual tasks, or preparation for later creation;
- students need to test, compare, and record, but not spend most of the lesson on a final work.

Expansion effect:

- explanation density: medium;
- demonstration density: medium;
- micro-practice count: 1-3;
- formal creation time: medium;
- evidence: trial sample, method note, comparison record, small work.

## High

Use `high` when:

- the lesson is a main creation, design, production, or project synthesis lesson;
- students need sustained work time and teacher circulation;
- the lesson contributes directly to a performance product or unit outcome.

Expansion effect:

- explanation density: low to medium;
- demonstration density: targeted;
- micro-practice count: 1-2, only as needed;
- formal creation time: high;
- evidence: process photo, draft, revision trace, finished or partial work, explanation of intent.

## Misuse Guard

Do not infer high practice merely because the subject is art. Appreciation, concept exploration, visual reading, and critique lessons may be low or medium practice.
