# R224A Unit Phase Role Registry

stage_id: 1013R_R224A_UNIT_LEVEL_PRACTICE_INTENSITY_ROUTER_PLANNING

## Registry

| unit_phase_role | Meaning | Typical lesson behavior | Expansion bias |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| intro_understanding | Introduces a concept, visual problem, question, or unit frame | Observe, compare, name, discuss, make a small record | More observation and guided talk, lighter creation |
| technique_preparation | Prepares a material, tool, method, or technique needed later | Demonstrate, short trial, compare results, record method | Medium practice with controlled trials |
| practice_creation | Main making / designing / experimenting lesson | Student work time, material choice, teacher circulation, revision | High practice, heavy teacher support and evidence |
| showcase_evaluation | Students present, compare, critique, and explain evidence | Display, peer feedback, self-evaluation, evidence selection | More evaluation and language support |
| transfer_closure | Connects learning to new context or life use | Review, transfer task, reflection, small extension | Medium or low practice, stronger synthesis |
| project_synthesis | Integrates multiple unit tasks into a performance product | Combine prior evidence, complete performance task, polish outcome | High coordination, evidence chain and task completion |

## Guard

`unit_phase_role` is not a decorative label. It must change classroom event expansion density.

Example:

- `intro_understanding` should not generate long creation support unless the source evidence requires it.
- `practice_creation` should not stay at surface-level observation if student practice and product evidence are central.
- `showcase_evaluation` should not be treated as ordinary end-of-class praise.
