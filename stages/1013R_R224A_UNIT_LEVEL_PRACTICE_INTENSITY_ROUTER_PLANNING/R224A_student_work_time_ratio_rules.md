# R224A Student Work Time Ratio Rules

stage_id: 1013R_R224A_UNIT_LEVEL_PRACTICE_INTENSITY_ROUTER_PLANNING

## Definition

`student_work_time_ratio` estimates the proportion of lesson time that should be given to student doing, making, trying, recording, discussing, or presenting.

## Low

Use when:

- lesson is mostly observation, appreciation, concept framing, or teacher-guided comparison;
- student work is short and evidence-light.

Typical effect:

- keep classroom events concise;
- avoid heavy practice scaffolds;
- focus evidence on observation and understanding.

## Medium

Use when:

- students need short trials, material tests, method comparison, small records, or brief expressive tasks;
- teacher still needs time for demonstration and shared comparison.

Typical effect:

- generate one or two micro-practice events;
- include learning sheet records;
- keep final product expectations modest.

## High

Use when:

- the lesson is a creation or project work lesson;
- students need time to draft, make, revise, collaborate, and present;
- teacher must circulate with observation and rescue points.

Typical effect:

- reduce teacher talk;
- include clear work-time blocks;
- generate circulation observation focus;
- preserve process evidence.

## Guard

`student_work_time_ratio` should not automatically equal `practice_intensity`, but they usually correlate. A high-practice lesson should rarely have low student work time unless the source evidence explicitly says so.
